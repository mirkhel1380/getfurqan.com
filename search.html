a starting point.

Arabic word searches match the source Quran text, ignoring vowel marks and common spelling variants. English results come from Quran.Foundation’s search index. A keyword result may match a translation or the Arabic text. Each result keeps its verse reference; open the reader for the full text and the translation you choose.

Search is not a fatwa or a complete interpretation. Use the published tafsir and surrounding passage for context. Personal notes are yours; they are never presented as a source explanation.
