Read your timetable with care.

These are calculated prayer start times for the selected city, not mosque congregation times. Friday prayer times depend on your mosque.

Calculation methods can differ by several minutes. Confirm the method and Asr setting with your local mosque or prayer calendar.

The year selector uses the Gregorian calendar. Hijri dates are calculated and may differ from local moon sighting.
