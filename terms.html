Using Al Furqan

These terms apply to getfurqan.online, operated as Al Furqan. Use the website lawfully and respectfully. Quran reading does not require an account. If you do not agree with these terms, stop using the service. Younger visitors should obtain parental or guardian permission where required by local law.

Religious content & careful study

Translations convey an understanding of the meanings and do not replace the Arabic Quran. Tafsir, hadith editions and grades must be read with their references and context. Editorial topics and personal notes are separate from published scripture and scholarly commentary. This website is a study aid, not a certified Mushaf or a personal religious ruling; consult a qualified scholar for individual questions.

We aim to display published texts accurately, but errors, missing entries and differences between editions can occur. Report suspected issues with the verse or hadith reference and edition name. Do not change or redistribute source text in a way that misrepresents its meaning or attribution.

Prayer times & personal records

Prayer times are calculated estimates based on your selected location and method, not a mosque congregation or Friday prayer timetable. Check the settings and local mosque guidance, especially at high latitudes. Hijri dates may differ from local moon sighting. Keep your own copies of notes and saved information; browser-only data may be lost, while optional private account sync requires a successful connection.
