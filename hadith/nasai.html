About the library, numbering and sources

This library indexes 36,512 numbered source entries across 10 collections. 36,108 have Arabic and/or English text; 404 have neither in these editions. Repeated narrations and versions are counted separately. This is the coverage of the available dataset, not a claim to contain every hadith in existence.

Some source book assignments are missing. Those entries remain within their collection and are labelled “Book not assigned in source”. Source numbering, including decimal identifiers, is preserved and can differ from other editions.

Texts and grading are supplied by the Hadith API dataset. Each report is loaded from a fixed source version and displayed here, without generated translations or grading. Individual grades are displayed where supplied; the presence of a report in a collection does not itself provide an individual grading.

Available language editions vary by collection. Translator or author attribution is shown as supplied by the dataset. Arabic and English coverage was checked; additional language editions are loaded on request and checked against the report reference.
