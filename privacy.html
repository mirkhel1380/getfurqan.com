Privacy Policy

How Al Furqan handles your information, account and browser data.

Who we are

Al Furqan operates getfurqan.online. This policy explains how this website uses information. You can read without an account. Contact us about privacy or your information using the email below.

Accounts & contact messages

For an account, we collect your name, email, account status and authentication records through Supabase. The administrator can view account details and manage access. Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are not visible in the admin panel.

The contact form stores your name, email, subject, message, website language, submission time and message reference. An authorized administrator uses these to answer and manage your enquiry. It does not subscribe you to marketing emails. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents.

Reading data & browser storage

Guest bookmarks, reading history, notes and reflections stay in your browser. After signing in, account reading data is stored separately on this device. Choosing Enable private sync gives permission to store your saved verses, reading place, daily goal, reading history, study notes and weekly reflections with Supabase and sync them across your signed-in devices. Guest data is imported only when you choose it. Language, theme, remembrance counters and prayer settings remain browser preferences; the prayer calendar cache is reused for up to 24 hours.

You can delete notes with their controls; when private sync is enabled, deletions reach your account when a sync succeeds. Clearing website data removes local copies and unsynced edits and may sign you out, but does not delete information already held in your account. Contact us to request server-held data deletion. Other people using the same browser profile may access local data. This website has no advertising trackers and does not sell personal information.

Weekly ayah view counts

When the weekly ayah is visible, we send its reference and a random browser token to count one visit per verse per day. The view-count table stores no email, account ID or IP address. Tokens older than 30 days are cleared when later visits are counted; aggregate totals remain. Hosting services may still receive connection data.

Voice search

Voice search starts only when you press its button and allow microphone access. Your browser may send audio to its own speech-recognition service under that service’s privacy practices. Al Furqan does not record or store raw microphone audio. Recognized text fills the search box for you to review; it is sent for a Quran search only when you submit it. Arabic and English voice input depend on browser support.

Service providers & international processing

GitHub Pages hosts the public website. Supabase supports accounts, contact messages and view counts; email services deliver account messages. Quran.Foundation, QuranEnc, the Hadith API dataset and its delivery services supply texts, fonts or audio. AlAdhan receives the city, country and calculation settings you select for prayer times; we do not request GPS location.

When a feature contacts a provider, that provider can receive your IP address, browser details and requested content. Providers may process information in other countries under their own policies. Authorized administrators and necessary service providers can access relevant records; disclosure may also be required by law. Contact us for information about applicable transfer safeguards.

Why we use information

We use information to provide requested features, authenticate accounts, answer enquiries, keep the service secure and prevent abuse. Where data protection law requires a legal basis, these purposes rely on providing the service you request and our legitimate interests in operating and securing it; we seek consent where required. We do not use your reading activity to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Retention & security

Account information remains while the account exists. Contact messages and administrative access records currently have no automatic expiry. We retain them for handling enquiries, follow-up and security, and you may request deletion. Limited records may need to remain for legal obligations or unresolved security issues; provider backups and logs follow their own retention arrangements.

We use access controls to restrict protected records. No online service or browser storage can guarantee complete security. Keep your password private, sign out on shared devices and keep copies of notes you want to preserve.

Your choices & privacy rights

You can update your profile name in your account. Email us to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your server-held information, or to raise a concern. Depending on applicable law, you may also request restriction, object to processing, withdraw consent where used, or complain to your local data protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Local browser notes cannot be retrieved by the administrator.

Younger visitors

Reading is open without registration. Younger visitors should involve a parent or guardian before creating an account or sending personal information where local law requires it. If a child supplied information without required permission, contact us to request its removal.

Policy updates

We update this page when our practices change and show the revision date above. Material changes will be brought to your attention where required. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to the address below.
