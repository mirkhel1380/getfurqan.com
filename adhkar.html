Know what
you are reading.

Hadith cards show the Arabic invocation excerpt and its collection reference. They do not include a generated translation or synthetic voice. Quran cards show the complete source verse, including any surrounding words, with a named published translation.

The 100-count remembrance follows its cited narration. Other checkmarks only track your reading; they do not set a religious repetition count. This is a starter collection, not a complete morning or evening litany. Specialist proofreading remains pending.
