WORSHIP · AN INTRODUCTION

Remembrance & dua

Dhikr is remembrance of Allah, and dua is calling upon Him. The Quran connects remembrance with gratitude and describes reassurance in the hearts of believers.

3 minute readQuran references included
IN THIS TOPICRemember and give thanksReassurance of the heartCalling upon AllahReferences & further reading← All topics

Remember and give thanks

Quran 2:152 brings remembrance and thankfulness together. Gratitude gives a direction to remembrance: recognising Allah’s gifts and responding with thanks.

Reassurance of the heart

Quran 13:28 describes believers whose hearts find reassurance in remembering Allah. Read this verse with the surrounding passage to understand its place in the surah.

Calling upon Allah

In Quran 2:186, Allah speaks of His nearness and answering the one who calls upon Him. When learning a particular remembrance or dua, keep its wording, context and reported count connected to its source. A decorative counter does not establish a religiously prescribed practice.

TO CARRY WITH YOU

A few things to remember.

  • Connect remembrance with gratitude.
  • Learn supplications together with their references.
  • Distinguish a personal counter from a reported prescription.
