DAILY LIFE · AN INTRODUCTION

Character & kindness

Kindness, restraint and respect are recurring themes in the Quran. They reach into the home, the neighbourhood and the way people speak to one another.

3 minute readQuran references included
IN THIS TOPICCare begins nearbyRestrain anger and forgiveRespect across differencesReferences & further reading← All topics

Care begins nearby

Quran 4:36 joins worship of Allah with good treatment of parents, relatives, orphans, those in need, neighbours, companions and travellers. Read the full verse to see the breadth of this responsibility.

Restrain anger and forgive

Quran 3:134 praises those who give in ease and hardship, restrain anger and pardon others. Character is expressed in the response to difficult moments as well as comfortable ones.

Respect across differences

Quran 49:13 describes humanity as peoples and tribes who may come to know one another. It places honour before Allah in righteousness, rather than ancestry or social status.

TO CARRY WITH YOU

A few things to remember.

  • Treat family, neighbours and people in need with care.
  • Practise restraint when anger rises.
  • Meet people without pride in status or ancestry.
